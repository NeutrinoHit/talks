# Prospects and closing figures

Generated lecture-style figures. Generator, tests and the full source table are
in `assets/prospects/README.md`. Sources were checked on 13 October 2026.

- `gvd-to-bhunt.svg`: published Baikal-GVD and HUNT milestones (JINR, 1 Apr 2025
  and 3 Mar 2026; baikal-gvd.jinr.ru; IHEP project page, 11 Oct 2025;
  PoS(ICRC2025)1101; Science and Technology Daily, 5 Feb 2025) and two cubes of
  1 and 30 km³ drawn to volume scale. Not a detector layout.
- `discovery-map.svg`, `discovery-map.png`: open questions on the neutrino-energy axis over
  the IceCube Combined-Fit spectrum (PRL 136, 121002 (2026)). Positions:
  NGC 1068 range (arXiv:2510.13403), spectral break (Table III), Baikal-GVD
  above 200 TeV (ApJ 982, 73 (2025)), Earth absorption above 40 TeV
  (Nature 551, 596 (2017)), Glashow resonance 6.3 PeV (Nature 591, 220 (2021)),
  KM3-230213A 220 PeV (Nature 638, 376 (2025)).
- `people-chain.svg`: symbolic chain ФНАФЧ («Физика нейтрино и астрофизика
  частиц», teach-in.ru) → people → results → B-HUNT. No names or photographs.
- `sources-sky.svg`, `sources-sky.png`: equatorial sky map with the Galactic plane,
  NGC 1068, TXS 0506+056 and the KM3-230213A direction (positions from
  arXiv:2603.21261v2, Table 1). Marks evidence, not fluxes or significances.
