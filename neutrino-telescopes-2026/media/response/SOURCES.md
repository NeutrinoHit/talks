# Detector response and compact-source diagram

- `icecube-directional-area-2026.png`: original `Definitions/eff-area.png`
  from [Spiering, arXiv:2609.26436v1 (2026), Fig. 6](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.26436v1).
  The review's illustrative IceCube point-source selection demonstrates energy
  and zenith dependence. It is not a calibrated current acceptance or a detector
  ranking. Original pixels, labels and curves are unchanged. Credit: C. Spiering.
- `icecube-hese-2013-effective-area.svg`: complete effective-area panel from
  `eff_area.pdf` in [IceCube, arXiv:1311.5238v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1311.5238v2).
  All-sky-averaged response of the historical contained-event sample, by flavour,
  with equal neutrino/antineutrino assumptions. Earth attenuation and the
  electron-antineutrino Glashow peak are included. Converted with Poppler, with
  no changes to scientific content. Credit: IceCube Collaboration.
- `compact-source.svg`: editable causal diagram generated by
  `assets/response/compact_source.py`. It depicts energetic hadrons, gas/photon
  targets, pion decay, neutrino escape and possible photon conversion/reprocessing.
  It is neither a source image nor a transport or luminosity calculation.
  Physics references: [Murase & Stecker](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.03381) and
  [IceCube NGC 1068](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.09972).

Published source figures retrieved 12 October 2026. Source/output identities
and checksums are in `assets/evidence/figures.json`.
