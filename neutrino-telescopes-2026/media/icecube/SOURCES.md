# IceCube's first PeV cascades

Official event displays, credited to IceCube Collaboration / IceCube–NSF.

- `bert-ernie.png`: the pair of event displays linked by the IceCube timeline,
  https://icecube.wisc.edu/about-us/icecube10/timeline/ .
- `big-bird.jpg`: the Big Bird event display linked by the same timeline.

The energies shown on slide 2 are deposited energies, rounded for orientation.
Bert and Ernie: 1.04 ± 0.16 and 1.14 ± 0.17 PeV, respectively, in
https://arxiv.org/abs/1304.5356 . Big Bird: approximately 2 PeV,
https://arxiv.org/abs/1405.5303 and
https://icecube.wisc.edu/news/research/2014/05/growing-astrophysical-neutrino-signal-in-icecube-now-features-2-pev-neutrino/ .

The cosmic-flux result uses the population and atmospheric-background analysis;
these three displays illustrate the signal rather than replacing that analysis.
Big Bird was observed in December 2012 and reported in the three-year analysis
in 2014. Do not call all three the original two-year discovery sample.
