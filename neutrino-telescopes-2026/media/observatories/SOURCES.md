# Observatory images and numerical conventions

## IceCube and IceCube-Gen2

`icecube-gen2-layout.png`: official design visualization from
[IceCube-Gen2 Detectors](https://icecube-gen2.wisc.edu/facility/detectors/).
Credit: **DESY, Science Communication Lab**. It shows the proposed expansion,
not the installed detector. The optical-array design instruments 8 km³.
The source page contains older Upgrade status text; the installed five-string
configuration comes instead from the
[12 February 2026 deployment announcement](https://icecube.wisc.edu/news/press-releases/2026/02/the-icecube-neutrino-observatory-gets-a-major-upgrade-beneath-the-ice/).

Original image:
<https://res.cloudinary.com/icecube/images/w_702,h_468,c_scale/q_auto/v1669229550/IceCube-Gen2/IceCube-Gen2/IceCube-Gen2.png?_i=AA>.
Educational use follows the collaboration's
[photo and multimedia guidelines](https://icecube.wisc.edu/press-guide/),
with the stated image credit retained.

## KM3NeT

`km3net-arca-orca.png`: the collaboration's
[full-design footprint comparison](https://www.km3net.org/research/detector/arca-and-orca-detector/).
Credit: **Courtesy KM3NeT**. The two ARCA blocks and denser ORCA block are
design configurations, not current installed-string counts.
Numerical sources: [ARCA](https://www.km3net.org/research/physics/astronomy-with-arca/)
and [ORCA](https://www.km3net.org/research/physics/particle-physics-with-orca/).
ORCA's approximately 7 Mt describes instrumented water mass.

Original image:
<https://www.km3net.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/KM3NeT-ARCA-and-ORCA.png>.
Reuse: [KM3NeT CC BY-NC 4.0 policy](https://www.km3net.org/pictures-and-videos/copyright/).

`km3net-artist-impression.jpg` (main image of the KM3NeT tab since 13 October 2026):
artist's impression "View from inside a detector building block of detection units",
credit **Edward Berbee/Nikhef**, from the KM3NeT
[Artist's impressions album](https://www.km3net.org/pictures-and-videos/picture-galleries/album-artists-impressions/).
Original: <https://www.km3net.org/wp-content/uploads/2016/07/Album-km3net-artist-expression.jpg>
(7820 × 4376, SHA-256 `0b577a82…7d3f`), downscaled to 2000 × 1119 (JPEG quality 88), no other change.
An illustration, not a photograph or an as-built configuration.
Reuse: [KM3NeT CC BY-NC 4.0 policy](https://www.km3net.org/pictures-and-videos/copyright/).
The top-view `km3net-arca-orca.png` above is kept for the backup section.

## Baikal-GVD

`baikal-gvd-2026.jpg`: **Конфигурация телескопа 2026**, from the collaboration's
[telescope description](https://baikal-gvd.jinr.ru/telescope).
Credit: **Baikal-GVD**. Original labels, deployment years and the separate
experimental cluster are retained. Sixteen physics clusters are shown.
The source's 0.8 km³ is explicitly an effective volume. It is not a
geometric volume or a universally defined response independent of energy.
No event-selection definition is supplied on that page.

Original image:
<https://hub.jinr.ru/images/gallery/20261009/large-photo-1726-1791536043-0.jpg>.
No individual illustrator or image-specific open licence is stated.

## P-ONE

`p-one-configuration.jpg`: official
[baseline configuration illustration](https://www.pacific-neutrino.org/p-one/).
Credit: **K. Holzapfel / P-ONE**. It depicts a proposed seven-cluster array
and a ten-string cluster. The text baseline specifies 70 strings and about
1 km³. Dimensions printed in the illustration belong to that schematic's
version, not a completed installation survey.
Deployment context: [ONC, 15 August 2026](https://www.oceannetworks.ca/news-and-stories/stories/onc-coengineers-key-infrastructure-for-pacific-deep-sea-neutrino-telescope/).

Original image:
<https://www.pacific-neutrino.org/fileadmin/user_upload/P-one_configuration.jpg>.
No image-specific open licence is stated on the source page.

## TRIDENT

`trident-layout.png`: Figure 2, **Geometrical layout of the TRIDENT array**,
in [Ye et al., Nature Astronomy (2023)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41550-023-02087-6).
Credit: **Ye et al. / TRIDENT**. Penrose-tiling full-array proposal,
1,211 strings and approximately 7.5 km³ geometric instrumented volume.
The scale bar and ROV maintenance paths are preserved.

Original image:
<https://media.springernature.com/lw685/springer-static/image/art%3A10.1038%2Fs41550-023-02087-6/MediaObjects/41550_2023_2087_Fig2_HTML.png>.
The article's Open Access licence is [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/); this figure has no separate
third-party credit line.

## HUNT

`hunt-design.jpg`: official concept illustration on
[IHEP's HUNT project page, 11 October 2025](https://ihep.cas.cn/astro/en/research/sf/ucp/202510/t20251011_7986821.html).
Credit: **HUNT / IHEP, CAS**. The page describes a proposed approximately
30 km³ telescope and tests in the South China Sea and Lake Baikal.
The image is an illustration, not an underwater photograph.
The 20-inch PMT technology is described by
[Peng et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.09910).
B-HUNT is not counted as a second independent HUNT capacity.

Original image:
<https://ihep.cas.cn/astro/en/research/sf/ucp/202510/W020251011650570947148_ORIGIN.jpg>.
No individual illustrator or image-specific open licence is stated.

## NEON

`neon-layout.png`: upper panel of Figure 3 from
[Zhang et al., arXiv:2408.05122v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.05122v3),
published as Astroparticle Physics 171 (2025) 103123.
Credit: **Zhang et al. / NEON**. Candidate array geometry and optical module,
not deployed hardware. The proposal specifies 10 km³ and 1,200 strings.
The original radius and height labels are retained.

Original PDF: <https://arxiv.org/pdf/2408.05122>.
The original 1920×1080 colour bitmap was extracted from PDF page 6 using
`pdfimages -f 6 -l 6 -png`; only the upper panel is included.
No redraw, composition, cropping or retouching was applied.

All other local images are unchanged source-image copies. Credits accompany
the figures and full scientific references are also in the slide notes.
