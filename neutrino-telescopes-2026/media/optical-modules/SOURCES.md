Collected for the English neutrino-telescope lecture on 2026-10-09 (Europe/Moscow). These are photographs of real hardware, not drawings or generated illustrations. Original pixels have been preserved: no compositing, retouching or speculative reconstruction. Source credits should remain in slide notes and, where practical, in visible image captions.

## TRIDENT

- Official source: [Tsung-Dao Lee Institute engineering-progress article](https://tdli.sjtu.edu.cn/post/15829), published 2025-10-12. Its title reports successful sea tests of the SPIDER deployment system; the article also describes the hDOM programme and contains the selected laboratory photograph directly after its explanation of hybrid PMT + SiPM technology.
- Original image: <https://tdli.sjtu.edu.cn/storage/wechat/f1edf479fa9cc7c302232226d27ed70e.jpg>.
- The image shows a completed transparent sphere with PMTs and interleaved small green SiPM assemblies in a laboratory. The source chronology reports a first fully functional hDOM in September 2025; the photograph is presented here only as a **laboratory prototype**, not as a claim about deployed array status or its eventual finalized configuration.
- Suggested credit: **TRIDENT / Tsung-Dao Lee Institute, SJTU**. No named photographer, image-specific licence or open redistribution permission was stated on this page. This is a credited educational reference copy; for broad reuse beyond DN's educational material obtain the collaboration's permission rather than implying a Creative Commons licence.
- Scientific/technology context: [Xin Xiang's official TDLI research page](https://web.tdli.sjtu.edu.cn/xxiang/research/) describes hDOM integration and the combination of PMTs and SiPMs.

## Selection notes

The four recommended photos were visually inspected. Trial downloads of unrelated expedition/booth scenes and small preview images were removed; no pre-existing material was deleted. No photograph has been recast as a simulation or vice versa. The P-ONE official [P-ONE-1 page](https://www.pacific-neutrino.org/main-menu/prototype) documents 16 PMTs per complete P-OM, but no P-ONE asset is included in this four-experiment comparison.
