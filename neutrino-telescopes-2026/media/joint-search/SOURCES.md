# Joint ANTARES + IceCube search, redrawn

`joint-discovery-flux-dark.svg`, `.png`: lecture-style redraw of the E^-2
5σ discovery-flux curves digitized from the vector Fig. 2 (left) of
[ANTARES & IceCube, ApJ 892, 92 (2020), arXiv:2001.04412](https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.04412).
Same quantity, units, declination range and samples. Gold: joint analysis;
blue dotted: IceCube 7 years; lavender dashed: ANTARES 9 years. Method and
tests: `assets/joint-search/README.md`. The original
`media/source-results/joint-discovery-flux.svg` is unchanged.
